The parser can warn that an empty-keystrokes wait command must end with a newline when followed by another command, even though the wait has no text to concatenate. In `parse_llm_response`, after parsing, any empty-keystrokes command that is followed by another command is rewritten to a single newline while preserving its duration. The matching false concatenation warning is removed, and the distinctive normalization trigger is appended to the warning feedback only when this rewrite occurs.
